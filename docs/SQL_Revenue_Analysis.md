## Executive Summary

The analysis estimates the potential revenue across New York City neighbourhood groups using listing price and annual availability.

Manhattan has the highest estimated revenue potential at approximately $576 million, followed by Brooklyn at approximately $280 million. Queens ranks third with approximately $86.49 million, while the Bronx and Staten Island have considerably lower estimated revenue potential.

The results indicate that Manhattan represents the largest potential revenue market in the dataset. This is likely influenced by its combination of a large number of listings and relatively high average listing prices.

## Business Recommendation

Airbnb should prioritize Manhattan and Brooklyn when developing revenue-focused strategies because these markets represent the largest estimated revenue potential.

The platform could focus on improving host performance, pricing optimization, and availability management in these high-value markets.

However, these figures should not be interpreted as actual revenue. The calculation uses:

Estimated Revenue Potential = Listing Price × Available Days

Availability does not indicate that a listing was actually booked. Therefore, actual booking, occupancy, and transaction data would be required to calculate real revenue.
