# Executive Summary

The analysis identifies the top 10 Airbnb hosts based on estimated revenue potential calculated using listing price and annual availability.

Sonder (NYC) has the highest estimated revenue potential at approximately $24.56 million, followed by Blueground at $18.02 million and Kara at $10.45 million.

The results show that high-volume hosts and professional property managers can represent a substantial portion of the potential economic value within the Airbnb marketplace.

However, these figures represent estimated revenue potential rather than actual revenue, because the dataset does not provide actual booking or occupancy data.

# Business Recommendation

Airbnb should identify and closely support high-value hosts because they may represent a significant portion of marketplace inventory and revenue potential.

The platform could provide high-volume hosts with enhanced analytics, account support, and property-management tools.

However, management should combine estimated revenue with actual booking/occupancy data, reviews, and cancellation information before making financial or strategic decisions.
