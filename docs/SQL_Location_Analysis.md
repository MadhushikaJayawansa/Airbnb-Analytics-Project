# Executive Summary

Staten Island has the highest average number of reviews per Airbnb listing, with approximately 30.94 reviews per listing. Queens ranks second with 27.70 reviews, followed by the Bronx with 25.98. Brooklyn and Manhattan have lower average review counts of 24.20 and 20.99 respectively.

The results indicate that listings in Staten Island receive relatively high levels of customer review activity compared with the other neighbourhood groups.

# Business Recommendation

Airbnb should investigate the factors contributing to the relatively high review activity in Staten Island and Queens. These factors could include listing characteristics, pricing, guest experience, or market conditions.

However, review counts should not be treated as a direct measure of bookings or demand because not every guest leaves a review. Airbnb should combine review activity with availability, pricing, and other performance metrics when evaluating neighbourhood performance.

# Executive Summary

The analysis shows significant differences in minimum-stay requirements across New York City neighbourhood groups. Manhattan has the highest average minimum stay at approximately 8.58 nights, followed by Brooklyn at 6.06 nights. The Bronx has the lowest average minimum stay at approximately 4.56 nights.

This suggests that listings in Manhattan are more likely to impose longer minimum booking requirements, while listings in the Bronx, Staten Island, and Queens generally allow shorter minimum stays.

# Business Recommendation

Airbnb and hosts should consider adapting minimum-stay requirements to local market conditions. Manhattan hosts may be targeting longer-stay customers such as business travelers or extended-stay visitors, while shorter minimum stays in the Bronx and other boroughs may provide greater flexibility for guests.

Hosts should compare minimum-stay requirements with price, availability, and review activity before changing their policies, rather than assuming that shorter or longer minimum stays automatically produce better performance.
