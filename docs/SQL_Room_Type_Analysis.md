## Business Question 13

## Question

What is the average number of reviews for each room type?

## Results

| Rank | Room Type       | Average Reviews |
| ---: | --------------- | --------------: |
|    1 | Shared room     |       **16.62** |
|    2 | Entire home/apt |       **22.84** |
|    3 | Private room    |       **24.10** |

# Executive Summary

Private rooms have the highest average number of reviews per listing, with approximately 24.10 reviews, followed by entire homes/apartments with 22.84 reviews. Shared rooms have the lowest average review count at approximately 16.62 reviews per listing.

The results indicate that private-room listings experience relatively high customer review activity compared with the other room types.

# Business Recommendation

Airbnb and hosts can use review activity to understand customer engagement across different room types. Private-room hosts may benefit from maintaining the factors that contribute to positive guest experiences and continued customer engagement.

However, review counts should be evaluated together with listing volume, pricing, and availability before making business decisions, since reviews do not directly represent the number of bookings.
