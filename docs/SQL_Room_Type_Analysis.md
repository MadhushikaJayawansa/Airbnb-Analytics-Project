# Business Interpretation

Entire homes/apartments have the longest average minimum stay requirement, at approximately 8.5 nights.

Private rooms have the shortest requirement, at approximately 5.4 nights, while shared rooms fall in between at approximately 6.4 nights.

This suggests that entire-home listings tend to require guests to commit to longer stays, while private-room listings are more flexible for shorter stays.

Be careful with the wording, though: this result tells us about host-set minimum-stay requirements, not the actual length of guests' stays.

#  Executive Summary

The analysis shows clear differences in minimum-stay requirements across Airbnb room types. Entire homes/apartments have the highest average minimum stay of approximately 8.51 nights, compared with 6.43 nights for shared rooms and 5.38 nights for private rooms.

This indicates that entire-home listings generally have stricter minimum-stay requirements than private and shared-room listings.

# Business Recommendation

Airbnb can use these findings to support room-type-specific pricing and booking strategies. Hosts offering entire homes may benefit from targeting longer-stay customers, while private-room hosts can attract guests looking for shorter and more flexible stays.

Airbnb could also monitor whether longer minimum-stay requirements are associated with higher availability, lower review activity, or different pricing patterns.
