# Business Requirements

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Airbnb listing data to provide business insights using SQL, Snowflake, and Power BI.

---

## Business Goal

Help Airbnb managers understand:

- Listing performance
- Price distribution
- Most active hosts
- Most popular locations
- Availability
- Customer review trends

---

## Stakeholders

- Airbnb Managers
- Business Analysts
- Regional Managers
- Marketing Team

---

## Business Questions

1. Which borough has the most listings?
2. Which neighborhoods have the highest number of listings?
3. What is the average listing price?
4. Which hosts own the most properties?
5. Which room type is most common?
6. How available are listings throughout the year?
7. Which listings receive the most reviews?

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Listings
- Average Price
- Average Reviews
- Average Minimum Nights
- Listings by Borough
- Top Hosts
- Available Listings
